Khmer Menu Intelligence

Ask for a budget and dishes. The app searches one restaurant at a time and cites every item as printed. If a want (like beer) is missing from a menu, you still get the rest — with the gap called out.

Exchange rate used: 4,000 KHR = 1 USD (fixed; no live rate). See assumptions in the repo.

What I don’t trust yet
